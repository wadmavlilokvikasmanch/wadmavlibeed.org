Our Story

Where Compassion Meets Action

Every meaningful journey begins with a simple belief—that every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and equal opportunity.

Founded on 19 June 2000 by Mr. Bhaskar Annasaheb Munde, a Post Graduate in Master of Social Work with over 27 years of experience, Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch has grown into a trusted organisation working for sustainable rural and community development across Maharashtra.

From empowering women through Self-Help Groups to providing education for children, supporting organic farming, conserving water, creating livelihood opportunities and responding during disasters, our work focuses on building self-reliant communities rather than offering temporary assistance.

"Real change begins when people are empowered with opportunity, guided by compassion and united through community."

Founder

Bhaskar Annasaheb Munde

Founder & Secretary

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch was founded on 19 June 2000 by Mr. Bhaskar Annasaheb Munde, a Post Graduate in Master of Social Work with over 27 years of experience in rural and community development.

His vision has always been simple yet powerful — to create opportunities where none existed and empower communities to become self-reliant with dignity, confidence and hope.

Today, under his leadership, the organisation continues to transform thousands of lives through education, women empowerment, sustainable agriculture, livelihood generation, water conservation and disaster relief.

"Towards Empowerment is not just our tagline — it is our commitment to every individual we serve."
