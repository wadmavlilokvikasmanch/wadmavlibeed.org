Beed district · October 2026
The Ground Reality

Farmers lost both their cash crops and their food crops.

Farmers had already spent money on seeds and inputs — often by taking loans. With almost no rain, they lost almost 100% of the Kharif harvest.

Normally, families earn around Diwali by selling soybean. This year, there is nothing to sell and no income to fall back on.

With Diwali approaching, families have little or no money in hand. This is a serious food and livelihood crisis.
