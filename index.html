WHO WE ARE

Working Towards Empowerment

Creating opportunities and strengthening communities through inclusive and sustainable development.

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch, popularly known as WLVM, was established on 19 June 2000. The organisation works for the development and empowerment of communities in need.

Our work includes education, women empowerment, skill development, livelihoods, sustainable agriculture, water conservation, environmental action and community development.

We believe development becomes stronger when people have knowledge, skills, resources and opportunities to shape their own future.
