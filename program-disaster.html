Supporting Beed District Hospital

During the critical phase of the pandemic, we provided 40 oxygen machines to Beed District Hospital to support patients in need. This initiative was made possible through the support of our dedicated partners and volunteers.

The oxygen machines played a vital role in saving lives during the second wave of COVID-19, when hospitals were facing acute shortages of medical supplies.

Recognition: We are proud to have received certificates of appreciation from the District Collector and Beed District Hospital for our COVID-19 relief efforts.
