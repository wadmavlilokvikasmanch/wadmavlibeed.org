🌸 About Us

Where Every Child Shines

Our Mission Education program creates a nurturing environment where children feel safe, happy, and excited to learn.

A Place of Joy and Discovery

Mission Education is our flagship program for children aged 6 to 13 years, operating in Kalepadal, Hadapsar, Pune. We believe that every child deserves a happy start to their learning journey.

Our approach combines playful learning with foundational education — we use stories, art, music, and hands-on activities to make learning fun and memorable for every child.
