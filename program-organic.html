Sustainable Agriculture for a Better Future 🌾 520+ Farmers

Wadmavli Lok Vikas Manch supports farmers in adopting organic farming practices through training, demos, group formation, government scheme awareness, and exposure visits. We are working with 520+ farmers across 10 villages in Beed district.

Chemical-Free Farming
🌱 Our Mission

Supporting Farmers Through Organic Farming

We work with farmers to adopt sustainable, chemical-free farming practices that improve soil health and livelihoods.

Building a Sustainable Future

Our Organic Farming program is designed to help farmers transition from chemical-intensive farming to natural, sustainable practices. We provide training, demonstrations, and ongoing support to ensure successful adoption.

With 520+ farmers across 10 villages, we are creating a network of organic farmers who share knowledge, resources, and best practices. Through exposure visits and group formation, we help farmers learn from each other and build a stronger organic farming community.

🌿 What We Do

Our Key Initiatives

We support farmers through every step of their organic farming journey.

🧑‍🌾

Training & Demos

Providing hands-on training and live demonstrations on organic farming techniques.

🤝

Group Formation

Helping farmers form groups for collective learning, resource sharing, and support.

🏛️

Government Scheme Awareness

Helping farmers access government schemes, subsidies, and support programs.

✈️

Exposure Visits

Organizing visits to successful organic farms for learning and inspiration.

🥬

Kitchen Garden

Promoting kitchen gardens for household food security and nutrition.
