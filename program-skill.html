❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our program.

❓ Who can join? ▼

Age 18-30 years, 12th pass or graduate. The program is completely free of cost.

💰 Is there any fee? ▼

No, the training is completely FREE. No fees are charged for any course.

📜 Will I receive a certificate? ▼

Yes, you will receive a recognized certificate upon successful completion of the training.

💼 Do you provide placement support? ▼

Yes, we provide job placement assistance and career guidance after training.

📋 What documents are required? ▼

Aadhaar card, 12th pass marksheet or graduation marksheet, ration card, and income certificate.

🏠 Do you provide accommodation? ▼

No, we do not provide accommodation. The training is centre-based.

⏰ Is there any age relaxation? ▼

No, the age limit is strictly 18-30 years.
