Safe Water for All
💧 Our Mission

Providing Clean Water to Every Community

We work tirelessly to ensure that every family has access to safe, clean drinking water.

Water is Life

Access to clean drinking water is a basic human right. Through our program, we have provided sustainable water solutions to communities in Beed district through wells, borewells, handpumps, and water storage tanks.

We have also supported communities during drought through tanker services and are now working with the Azim Premji Foundation to bring clean water to 3 more villages in the coming years.

🔮 Future Plan

Expanding Water Access with Azim Premji Foundation

3 vastis in 3 villages of Beed taluka will be provided with clean drinking water sources.

Bringing Clean Water to Every Home

With the support of the Azim Premji Foundation, we are planning to provide clean drinking water sources to 3 vastis in 3 villages of Beed taluka over the next 3 years.

This initiative will include the construction of wells, borewells, and the installation of pipelines in the middle of the vastis with water storage tanks, ensuring that every family has access to safe drinking water.
