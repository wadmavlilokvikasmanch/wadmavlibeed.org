🌊 Our Mission

Protecting Water Resources for Future

We work with communities to conserve water and protect land through sustainable watershed practices.

Building a Water-Secure Future

Watershed development is at the heart of our water conservation efforts. Through scientific and community-led approaches, we are helping farmers and communities secure water for agriculture and daily needs.

From constructing farm ponds and band bandasti to implementing dagdi band and chaur khodne, we are creating sustainable water solutions that benefit both people and the environment.

⏳ Past Work

Supporting Farmers During Drought 2015-16

During the severe drought, we provided labour and water conservation support to 20 farmers.

Standing with Farmers in Crisis

During the devastating drought of 2015-16, we worked with 20 farmers to provide labour support and water conservation measures. We dug wells and implemented water-saving techniques to help farmers sustain their livelihoods during the crisis.
