🌸 Our Mission

Empowering Women Through Self Help Groups

Since 2000, we have been creating opportunities for women to become self-reliant and confident.

Building Confidence & Independence

Our Women Self Help Group program is designed to empower women from rural communities through collective action, financial literacy, and skill development.

With monthly meetings, CLFs (Cluster Level Federations), and CFS (Community Federation Structures), we ensure that every woman has a voice and access to opportunities for growth and self-reliance.

From SHG formation to entrepreneur support, we walk with women every step of the way — helping them build businesses, access government schemes, and create a better future for themselves and their families.
